About

Hi, I'm Pietro, a senior software engineer passionate about early-stage products, especially at the intersection of audio / video production and technology.

I also created autoEdit, the first digital paper editing app that uses audio and video interview transcripts to craft your story.

I also take photos↗, film documentaries and write up 'how-tos' "Tips Tricks & Quick Fix" and blog posts.
